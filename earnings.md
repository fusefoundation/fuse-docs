# Operator earnings

An active interface receives **40% of the protocol fee** on eligible real daily contributions attributed to it. It does not receive 40% of all deposits or of the pooled funds.

For example, if an attributed participant’s **0.10 ckUSDT** daily contribution is used in a round:

- The 10% protocol fee is **0.01 ckUSDT**.
- The interface share is 40% of that fee: **0.004 ckUSDT**.

A top-up by itself does not earn the operator share. Only the attributed daily contributions that are actually used count. Virtual reserve contributions do not earn an interface share.

Use the operator cabinet to check your accrued and available amounts. Before requesting a payout, check the receiving account, any applicable ledger fee and the net amount shown. A pending or uncertain payout should be checked before attempting another one.
